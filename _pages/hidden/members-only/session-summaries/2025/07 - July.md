## July 3, 2025

- Participants: Richard, Anuj, Dylan, Sean
- Area: Labyrinth Area

Sean and I went over to the (newly named) Three Willows Meadow after the session so that Sean could photograph the Eastern Tiger Swallowtail larva on an ash tree. Afterwards, we spent a few minutes with the UV flashlights and found another Waved Sphinx, ten more Delicate Cycnia, and a small leaf folder on a goldenrod. These have been added to the session summary.


BTW, the leaf blotch mines we saw on the catalpa leaves were caused by the larva of *Amauromyza pleuralis*, a species of fly.

## July 18, 2025

- Participants: Richard, Clara, Jeff, Sean
- Areas: OURSPace, Locust Alley 

Despite spending quite a bit of time looking at specimens I brought with me and returning the Gray Furcula to the tree I got it from, we still managed to find 13 species of caterpillars in the time remaining. Of the three unidentified species of geometers I brought home with me, I may need to return the two tiny ones to the park since they are moving around the vial non-stop and risk burning more calories than they are consuming. I will wait a few more hours to see if they calm down and start focusing on feeding.

BTW, the pupa that Jeff found near the end of the session (it was in a silk casing on the underside of a sugar maple leaf (not Norway maple as I said at the time), turned out to be empty.
