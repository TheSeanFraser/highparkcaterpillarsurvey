## May 25, 2025

- Participants: Richard, Jason, Sean
- Area(s): OURSpace, Three Willows

We had around 9 species. I say "around" since I am not sure exactly how many species of cutworms we saw, but I am going to put the number of unknown cutworm species at three. Quantities of the cutworms are estimates.

The hairstreak was a Banded Hairstreak. The only other record we have for this species is from June 1, 2023 when I found one a wooden railing in the dog off-leash corridor. So, nice find! Banded Hairstreak feeds on various species of oak, hickory, and walnut. The fact that we found it on a goldenrod shows that we sometimes find caterpillars on plants that aren't their hosts.

I will try rearing The White-speck to see if that's what it really is.
